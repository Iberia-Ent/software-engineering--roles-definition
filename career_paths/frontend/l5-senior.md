# Frontend Developer · L5 Senior

[Academy index](../README.md) · [Frontend overview](./README.md)

## Purpose

Explain the wider technical direction, mentoring, and influence expected at Senior level while
keeping the source's pending final promotion calibration explicit.

## Audience

Product Owner, Product Manager, Delivery Lead, and Technical Lead. Frontend Developer, Coach,
Mentor, and Architect use the chapter for technical calibration.

## Overview

L5 focuses on technical direction, responsibility, coaching, and influence beyond the immediate
team. The source includes design systems, security leadership, accessibility standards, and
structured mentoring. Its final L5 promotion gate is explicitly still awaiting calibration with
Technical Leads and the client.

## Product-team context

A representative contribution could be guiding a shared Frontend pattern or standard that
multiple product teams adopt. Stakeholders should understand why the investment matters, which
delivery or customer risks it addresses, and how the recommendation fits current product needs.
The value lies in adoption and outcomes, not simply producing a design document.

Coaching should develop others' ability to make sound decisions and deliver effectively. Senior
contribution should make teams less dependent on one expert, while retaining clear ownership of
the consequences of technical choices. Broader influence does not remove the need to collaborate
with product and technical stakeholders.

## Evidence stakeholders can discuss

Bring examples of technical direction that influenced architecture or product decisions,
structured growth support for another Developer, and standards used in real delivery. Ask what
changed for the team and what trade-offs were made. These discussion prompts interpret the
source criteria and must not be used as substitute approval rules.

## Detailed criteria

The criteria below are reproduced from the English career-path chapter and grouped
by area. They describe technical expectations; the product interpretation explains
why they matter to delivery and does not add a separate promotion threshold.

### Common technical criteria (both tracks)

<a id="detail-l5-common"></a>

[Source criteria](./Frontend_Career_Path_Chapter.md#criteria-l5-common-technical-criteria-both-tracks)

Evaluate whether technical direction improves multiple teams' ability to deliver: API design for
developer experience, governed design systems, threat modelling, systemic debt remediation,
accessibility standards, and structured mentoring. Ask what was adopted and how teams benefited.

- **JavaScript/TypeScript:** iterators, generators, practical V8 optimisation, library APIs designed for the team's DX. <a id="item-l5-common-technical-criteria-both-tracks-javascripttypescript"></a>
- **Design Systems at scale:** compound components, cross-platform tokens, design system governance. <a id="item-l5-common-technical-criteria-both-tracks-design-systems-at-scale"></a>
- **Security leadership:** threat modelling, OWASP Top 10 in code reviews, TLS certificate management. <a id="item-l5-common-technical-criteria-both-tracks-security-leadership"></a>
- **Advanced Scrum:** critical judgement, facilitating ceremonies, identifying systemic technical debt with remediation plans (ROI). <a id="item-l5-common-technical-criteria-both-tracks-advanced-scrum"></a>
- **A11y:** defining and defending project standards, cross-cutting WCAG 2.1 AA, understanding AAA and when to pursue it. <a id="item-l5-common-technical-criteria-both-tracks-a11y"></a>
- **Active mentoring:** a growth plan for at least 1 Developer, active follow-up, structured knowledge transfer. <a id="item-l5-common-technical-criteria-both-tracks-active-mentoring"></a>

### Track-specific criteria

#### Track A (SPA Legacy)

<a id="detail-l5-track-a"></a>

[Source criteria](./Frontend_Career_Path_Chapter.md#criteria-l5-track-a-spa-legacy)

For React or Angular, discuss scaling and migration decisions across teams, informed performance
practice, and compatibility with legacy applications. The senior demonstrates a reasoned
standard and enables teams to apply it rather than becoming the only person who can solve the
problem.

- **React:** Profiler for re-renders, scalable design systems, appropriate use of React Server Components. <a id="item-l5-track-a-spa-legacy-react"></a>
- **Angular:** SSR with Angular Universal, AngularJS → Angular migration, module architecture for large teams; legacy AngularJS knowledge (`$scope`, digest cycle, directives). <a id="item-l5-track-a-spa-legacy-angular"></a>

#### Track B (Astro + AEM)

<a id="detail-l5-track-b"></a>

[Source criteria](./Frontend_Career_Path_Chapter.md#criteria-l5-track-b-astro-aem)

Review influence on AEM at scale, content strategy, security leadership, real-user performance,
and organisation-wide direction. Tie each claim to outcomes across products; a Track B
specialist should explain the effect on authors, customers, and delivery teams.

- AEM at scale. <a id="item-l5-track-b-astro-aem-aem-at-scale"></a>
- Content strategy leadership. <a id="item-l5-track-b-astro-aem-content-strategy-leadership"></a>
- Security leadership. <a id="item-l5-track-b-astro-aem-security-leadership"></a>
- Performance expert (RUM). <a id="item-l5-track-b-astro-aem-performance-expert-rum"></a>
- Vision setting. <a id="item-l5-track-b-astro-aem-vision-setting"></a>
- Org influence. <a id="item-l5-track-b-astro-aem-org-influence"></a>

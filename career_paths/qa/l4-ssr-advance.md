# QA Engineer · L4 SSR Advance

[Career Paths index](../README.md) · [QA overview](./README.md)

## Purpose

Explain L4 ownership of complex QA scope and the practice-multiplier impact product stakeholders
should observe beyond the person's own testing activities.

## Audience

Product Owner, Product Manager, Delivery Lead, and Technical Lead. QA Manager, QA Engineer,
Coach, and Mentor validate the linked technical and leadership criteria.

## Overview

L4 improves quality maturity at squad or domain level, mentors others, and influences decisions.
The November matrix describes a reference who strengthens reusable practices, guides adoption,
and brings systemic risks into the quality strategy.

## Product-team context

For example, the QA Engineer may coordinate coverage for a change that spans several components
or help the squad introduce reusable performance and accessibility checks. Stakeholders should
understand which risks the work addresses, why the chosen coverage is proportionate, and what
evidence is needed before recommending delivery.

The distinction from L3 is multiplication of capability, not just handling more tests. Coaching,
useful dashboards, improved quality gates, and adopted proof-of-concept work should help other
people make stronger quality decisions. Influence should be visible in practice, including how
disagreements about risk are handled.

## Evidence stakeholders can discuss

Review a complex-scope strategy, a practice adopted by peers, and a mentoring or improvement
outcome. Ask whether the change reduced avoidable duplication or made risk clearer, rather than
assuming every new automation asset adds value. These are evidence prompts, not new targets.

# QA Engineer · L5 Senior

[Career Paths index](../README.md) · [QA overview](./README.md)

## Purpose

Explain Senior QA impact across products, domains, and suppliers, with clear distinctions
between the current competency matrix and the older chapter's pending calibration statement.

## Audience

Product Owner, Product Manager, Delivery Lead, and Technical Lead. QA Manager, QA Engineer,
Coach, and Mentor validate the technical and organisational evidence.

## Overview

L5 sets quality direction and standards across products, domains, and suppliers. The November
matrix expects strategic influence, capability uplift, and standards for areas such as
performance, accessibility, observability, and responsible AI use.

## Product-team context

A representative contribution could align quality evidence and prioritisation principles across
teams that previously applied incompatible practices. Product stakeholders should understand the
rationale, adoption effort, and consequences for delivery confidence. A standard should help
teams make decisions, not simply add administrative work.

Senior impact includes mentoring, supplier alignment, and assessing proposals before they become
shared tools or AI-enabled assets. The person should shape a quality culture where teams can
challenge weak conditions with evidence and adapt good practices responsibly.

## Evidence stakeholders can discuss

Look for standards used across teams, capability growth in peers, and quality decisions improved
through cross-product collaboration. Discuss both adoption and outcomes: a presentation or
proposal alone does not demonstrate sustained organisational influence.

The criteria below reproduce the level-specific expectations from the November 2026
competency matrix. The interpretation after each area connects them to product-team
discussions; it is explanatory guidance, not an additional promotion gate.

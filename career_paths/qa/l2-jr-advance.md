# QA Engineer · L2 Jr Advance

[Academy index](../README.md) · [QA overview](./README.md)

## Purpose

Explain L2 expectations for reliable recurring QA work and help stakeholders recognise the move
from guided execution towards structured, increasingly independent contribution.

## Audience

Product Owner, Product Manager, Delivery Lead, and Technical Lead. QA Manager, QA Engineer,
Coach, and Mentor validate the detailed competency evidence.

## Overview

L2 applies QA fundamentals consistently in day-to-day work and begins contributing analysis, not
only execution. The November matrix describes limited supervision, basic risk-based
prioritisation, and active participation in refinement and testing discussions.

## Product-team context

For example, the QA Engineer may design coverage for a standard story, explain the most
important flows, and identify an obvious gap in the acceptance criteria. The discussion should
connect test effort with business risk rather than assume everything should be automated.
Guidance remains appropriate for unfamiliar scenarios and more complex decisions.

Stakeholders should see clearer reasoning about coverage, more active collaboration, and
reliable follow-through on recurring QA work. The approved automation stack is a means of
obtaining useful evidence, not a goal in itself. Performance, accessibility, cloud, and
observability knowledge should start appearing in guided practical work.

## Evidence stakeholders can discuss

Review a test design, the reason for its priorities, an identified requirements gap, and a
maintained automated asset where applicable. Ask whether the person understood why the chosen
checks mattered and when they needed support. Do not equate script counts or time served with
readiness for independent QA ownership.

## Detailed competencies

The criteria below reproduce the level-specific expectations from the November 2026
competency matrix. The interpretation after each area connects them to product-team
discussions; it is explanatory guidance, not an additional promotion gate.

### Test Design & Prioritization
<a id="detail-l2-test-design-prioritization"></a>

[Exact source criteria](./qa-career-path.md#criteria-test-design-prioritization-l2-jr-advance-foundation-guided-practice)

**Level snapshot:** Designs standard test cases with basic risk-based prioritization

Discuss why the selected flows and scenarios matter to customers and the business. The purpose
is to make coverage proportionate to risk and change impact, rather than equate more test cases
with more confidence.

### Automation & Tooling
<a id="detail-l2-automation-tooling"></a>

[Exact source criteria](./qa-career-path.md#criteria-automation-tooling-l2-jr-advance-foundation-guided-practice)

**Level snapshot:** Builds simple scripts and maintains automated assets

Consider whether the chosen automation gives maintainable, trustworthy evidence for recurring
product behaviour. Tool familiarity matters when it serves quality; script volume alone does not
demonstrate value.

- [Contributes to maintenance of automated assets in the approved stack](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l2-automation-tooling-01)
- [Starts building simple scripts with guidance using Karate, WebdriverIO, and TypeScript](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l2-automation-tooling-02)
- [Follows defined conventions and reusable patterns](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l2-automation-tooling-03)
- [Understands the need to adapt to new approved tools over time](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l2-automation-tooling-04)

### Quality Ownership / Authority
<a id="detail-l2-quality-ownership-authority"></a>

[Exact source criteria](./qa-career-path.md#criteria-quality-ownership-authority-l2-jr-advance-foundation-guided-practice)

**Level snapshot:** Challenges obvious gaps, does not accept weak deliverables

Make space for the QA Engineer to raise uncomfortable evidence. Stakeholders should distinguish

### AI-Enabled QA
<a id="detail-l2-ai-enabled-qa"></a>

[Exact source criteria](./qa-career-path.md#criteria-ai-enabled-qa-l2-jr-advance-foundation-guided-practice)

### Performance Testing
<a id="detail-l2-performance-testing"></a>

[Exact source criteria](./qa-career-path.md#criteria-performance-testing-l2-jr-advance-foundation-guided-practice)

**Level snapshot:** Guided scenarios and basic K6 scripts

Relate scenarios and results to expected product usage, response-time objectives, and customer
impact. A script passing is not itself a product-quality conclusion; interpretation and agreed
thresholds matter.

- [Turns simple stories into performance scenarios (users, ramp-up, duration, think time, acceptance criteria) with review](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l2-performance-testing-01)
- [Writes basic K6 scripts (requests, checks, thresholds, stages) with guidance](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l2-performance-testing-02)
- [Chooses the right test type for the change being delivered](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l2-performance-testing-03)
- [Reads p90/p95, error rate and throughput against the agreed SLOs](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l2-performance-testing-04)

### Accessibility (A11y)
<a id="detail-l2-accessibility-a11y"></a>

[Exact source criteria](./qa-career-path.md#criteria-accessibility-a11y-l2-jr-advance-foundation-guided-practice)

**Level snapshot:** Runs axe-core scans and basic screen reader checks

Connect checks to the people and journeys the product must support, and to the applicable
compliance evidence. Automated findings are useful but do not replace the manual checks
described by the source.

- [Runs automated scans (axe-core) and understands the reported violations](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l2-accessibility-a11y-01)
- [Uses screen readers at a basic level (NVDA, VoiceOver, TalkBack)](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l2-accessibility-a11y-02)
- [Selects the applicable A11y tests for a story based on its triggers](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l2-accessibility-a11y-03)
- [Reports A11y defects with WCAG criterion, severity and evidence](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l2-accessibility-a11y-04)

### AWS & Cloud Awareness
<a id="detail-l2-aws-cloud-awareness"></a>

[Exact source criteria](./qa-career-path.md#criteria-aws-cloud-awareness-l2-jr-advance-foundation-guided-practice)

**Level snapshot:** Follows technical conversations with Devs, TLs and Cloud

Use cloud knowledge to identify how architecture, environment differences, or asynchronous flows
change test scope. QA is expected to collaborate with specialists, not replace Cloud or
Architecture implementation ownership.

- [Follows technical conversations with Devs, TLs and Cloud teams in refinement and dailies](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l2-aws-cloud-awareness-01)
- [Understands how the architecture of a story (synchronous APIs, queues, events, storage) affects what and how to test](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l2-aws-cloud-awareness-02)
- [Asks the right questions when a change touches cloud components](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l2-aws-cloud-awareness-03)

### Observability (Dynatrace)
<a id="detail-l2-observability-dynatrace"></a>

[Exact source criteria](./qa-career-path.md#criteria-observability-dynatrace-l2-jr-advance-foundation-guided-practice)

**Level snapshot:** Uses Dynatrace during test execution and follows traces

Consider whether the team can use traces, dashboards, and signals to understand behaviour during
testing and make timely quality decisions. Observability should prevent surprises, not only
explain incidents after release.

- [Uses Dynatrace during test execution to check errors, response times and failure rates of the services under test](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l2-observability-dynatrace-01)
- [Follows a distributed trace to locate where a request fails](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l2-observability-dynatrace-02)
- [Compares behaviour between environments before raising a defect](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l2-observability-dynatrace-03)

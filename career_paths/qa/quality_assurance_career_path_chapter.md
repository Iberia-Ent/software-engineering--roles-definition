## Purpose

This document provides the professional growth framework for Quality Assurance (QA). It aligns
expectations across Technical Leads, Product Owners, Coaches, Managers, and QA Engineers, and
provides clear, objective criteria for promotion decisions.

> A level is considered consolidated when the professional demonstrates it consistently in
> daily work, not just occasionally or in isolated situations.

Evaluation draws on three sources:

1. **Role description:** Official position catalogue (My Growth).
2. **Soft skills:** Cross-cutting competencies for the QA career.
3. **Technical knowledge:** Based on project needs.

## Audience

This chapter is intended primarily for the Product Owner, Product Manager, Delivery Lead, and
Technical Lead supporting QA growth in a product team. QA leadership, Coach, and Mentor validate
the discipline-specific evidence. Product stakeholders contribute observations about delivery,
communication, and risk handling, not independent certification of tools or technical methods.

## Overview

This is the English translation of the May 2026 chapter. It complements the
[November 2026 matrix](./qa-career-path.md), which provides more granular competency expectations.
The documents do not use identical supervision language at every level, and this chapter's L5
gate is pending calibration. Discuss material differences with QA leadership rather than
combining them into an undocumented rule.

The responsibilities below should be read in the context of actual work and the support available.
A professional's ability to explain why an activity protects the product is as relevant to the
discussion as whether they can perform the activity. Technical confirmation remains necessary.

## Technical knowledge and tools

The career track is developed collaboratively within the discipline and covers technical and
methodological knowledge:

- **Methods and approaches:** Agile, shift-left testing, early analysis through test case design, execution, and defect reporting.
- **Languages and frameworks:** Java, TypeScript, WebdriverIO, Karate.
- **Automation:** Mobile automation (for example, Appium), Frontend automation, and Backend approaches.
- **Tools and practices:** Git (version control, commits, pushes), Jira (pass/fail/blocked statuses, defect reports with evidence and appropriate severity).
- **Patterns and design:** Page Object Model (POM), BDD with Gherkin (Given, When, Then).
- **APIs:** HTTP methods (GET, POST, PUT, DELETE) and JSON or XML data structures.
- **Infrastructure and cloud:** AWS (EC2, S3, IAM, Lambda), IaaS, PaaS, performance testing, and throughput.
- **Other:** Web accessibility (WCAG).

## L1: Junior

### Criteria and responsibilities

- **Supervision:** Works under continuous supervision, with a lead and/or Mentor reviewing tasks and progress daily or weekly.
- **Execution:** Works with the project team and needs clear task definitions, frequent reviews, and guidance when moving between tasks.
- **Management:** Needs support to prioritise tasks and use available time effectively.
- **Learning:** Faces a steep learning curve and should regularly spend time on learning activities, for example with a Coach.
- **Onboarding:** Must learn internal tools, company processes, and policies.

### Soft skills

| Competency | Observable indicators |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Aptitude** | Learns quickly, retains concepts, and absorbs stack fundamentals. Attends meetings punctually, meets short deadlines, asks promptly when blocked, and shows initiative. |
| **Reporting** | Communicates status clearly in daily standups without omitting blockers. Reports progress and difficulties when requested. |
| **Creative** | Suggests alternatives to a problem, even without making the final decision. |
| **Team Player** | Collaborates smoothly, accepts feedback without becoming defensive, and contributes to a positive team environment. |

**Promotion gate:** 80% Hard Skills · 100% Soft Skills

## L2: Jr Advanced

### Criteria and responsibilities

- **Supervision:** Works under continuous supervision while starting to take ownership and act autonomously on specific tasks.
- **Execution:** Needs clear task definitions, frequent reviews, and guidance when moving between tasks.
- **Management:** Needs support to prioritise and use available time effectively, with follow-up from a QA Champion or Senior.

### Soft skills

| Competency | Observable indicators |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Goal Oriented** | Understands each task's purpose and adapts execution to achieve the outcome, focusing on the deliverable rather than just completing steps. |
| **Problem Solving** | Investigates before escalating, suggests at least one solution when asking for help, and tries alternatives before remaining blocked. |
| **Analytical** | Breaks complex problems into manageable parts, reviews others' code with sound judgement, and identifies patterns. |
| **Documentary** | Provides README files, relevant comments, and clear descriptions that explain technical decisions. |

**Promotion gate:** 80% Hard Skills · 100% Soft Skills

## L3: Semi-Senior

### Criteria and responsibilities

- **Supervision:** Works with supervision and in close contact with the team lead and/or specialist.
- **Processes:** Helps review existing processes and proposes changes when needed.
- **Stakeholders:** Interacts more frequently with key stakeholders.
- **Reporting:** Is expected to report task status proactively.

### Soft skills

| Competency | Observable indicators |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Communication** | Explains technical decisions clearly to Technical Leads and less experienced QA Engineers, including in standups, retrospectives, and documents. |
| **Pressure Tolerance** | Remains calm and focused during critical sprints or scope changes without degrading work quality under pressure. |
| **Flexibility** | Adapts solutions when requirements change without prolonged blockers and reprioritises smoothly. |
| **Sense of Urgency** | Prioritises correctly, identifies blocking risks, addresses them first, and escalates promptly when necessary. |

**Promotion gate:** 80% Hard Skills · 100% Soft Skills

<a id="criteria-l4-ssr-advanced"></a>

## L4: SSR Advanced

### Criteria and responsibilities

- **Supervision:** Works with less supervision than a Semi-Senior and stays in close contact with the team lead and/or specialist.
- **Processes:** Helps review existing processes and proposes changes when needed.
- **Reporting:** Is expected to report task status less frequently.

### Soft skills

| Competency | Observable indicators |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Autonomy** | Delivers large 1–2 week features without daily check-ins, resolves or appropriately de-escalates blockers, and makes technical decisions within scope without constant validation. |
| **Negotiation** | Negotiates scope, deadlines, or technical debt with stakeholders using clear arguments, data, and alternatives. |

**Promotion gate:** 80% Hard Skills · 100% Soft Skills

## L5: Senior

### Criteria and responsibilities

- **Supervision:** Works across all development lifecycle phases with little or no technical supervision.
- **Problem resolution:** Assesses challenging situations to understand the underlying problems.
- **Delivery:** Takes responsibility for delivering complex tasks on time and within the team's agreed scope.
- **Leadership:** Leads collaboration with colleagues and guides Junior and Semi-Senior Analysts.
- **Advanced testing:** Tests code design, from small software components to complete flows, and reviews performance.
- **Community:** Attends conferences and participates in local email exchanges.

### Soft skills

| Competency | Observable indicators |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Responsibility** | Takes responsibility for technical decisions, raises urgent issues with the team, and owns the product or area end to end. |
| **Conflict Resolution** | Guides technical disagreements towards documented, objective decisions and mediates opposing positions with sound judgement. |
| **Coaching** | Develops a growth plan for at least one Developer or QA Engineer, practises active listening, and transfers knowledge in a structured way. |

> **Promotion gate:** Status: WIP — Pending final calibration with Technical Leads and the client.

## Evaluation and follow-up

| Source | Contribution to the process |
| :--- | :--- |
| **PO, SM, TL and Senior Developers** | Monthly structured feedback on observable behaviour: delivery, communication, team attitude, blocker management, and work quality. |
| **Stack Coach** | Validates each level's technical criteria, checking that the Developer or QA Engineer understands the work rather than simply executing it. Their input is decisive for approval. |
| **Mentor** | Globant supports broader career development: personal goals, cultural alignment, and organisational visibility. |

## Glossary

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Shift-left testing** | A QA approach that moves testing to the earliest development lifecycle phases. |
| **POM** | Page Object Model: a design pattern for object repositories (UI elements) in automation. |
| **BDD / Gherkin** | Behaviour-Driven Development: natural language (Given, When, Then) used to define tests and behaviour. |
| **Demonstrated** | Consistent in daily work (80%+ of the time). |
| **Soft Skills** | Behavioural (communication, ownership, leadership). |
| **Hard Skills** | Technical (frameworks, methods, automation). |
| **L1–L5** | Career level (Junior → Senior). |

# QA Engineer · L3 Semi Senior

[Career Paths index](../README.md) · [QA overview](./README.md)

## Purpose

Explain what autonomous QA ownership means for a product squad and provide direct access to the
competency criteria that underpin that responsibility.

## Audience

Product Owner, Product Manager, Delivery Lead, and Technical Lead. QA Manager, QA Engineer,
Coach, and Mentor validate technical proficiency and quality judgement.

## Overview

L3 is the proficiency point in the November matrix: the QA Engineer applies every competency
autonomously and owns the squad's QA scope for standard initiatives. This includes prioritising
by risk, explaining coverage, and making evidence-based quality recommendations.

## Product-team context

A representative situation is a release discussion in which the QA Engineer explains what was
tested, what remains uncertain, and how the remaining risks affect critical flows. They should
be able to reject weak conditions or escalate with evidence, rather than silently accepting
pressure to proceed. That quality authority does not remove the need for collaborative
decisions.

Performance, accessibility, cloud architecture awareness, and proactive Dynatrace use should
inform normal QA work. The person should also contribute beyond individual execution through the
community of practice and improvement proposals. Autonomous ownership means reasoned action
within scope, not working in isolation.

## Evidence stakeholders can discuss

Bring a risk-based coverage decision, a clear go/no-go recommendation, an escalation supported
by evidence, and a contribution shared with QA peers. Technical reviewers should validate
independent use of the approved stack and the four cross-cutting competency areas.

## Detailed competencies

The criteria below reproduce the level-specific expectations from the November 2026
competency matrix. The interpretation after each area connects them to product-team
discussions; it is explanatory guidance, not an additional promotion gate.

### Role Focus
<a id="detail-l3-role-focus"></a>

[Exact source criteria](./qa-career-path.md#criteria-role-focus-l3-semi-senior-proficient-autonomous)

**Level snapshot:** Owns QA scope as a real squad QA owner

Connect this scope to the squad's delivery model. Stakeholders should be able to describe what
QA owns at this level, what still needs support, and how the work contributes to confidence in
the product.

- [Own QA scope for standard initiatives](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l3-role-focus-01)
- [Contribute proactively to planning, prevention, and quality confidence](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l3-role-focus-02)

### Test Design & Prioritization
<a id="detail-l3-test-design-prioritization"></a>

[Exact source criteria](./qa-career-path.md#criteria-test-design-prioritization-l3-semi-senior-proficient-autonomous)

**Level snapshot:** Defines coverage by risk, change impact and critical flows

Discuss why the selected flows and scenarios matter to customers and the business. The purpose
is to make coverage proportionate to risk and change impact, rather than equate more test cases

- [Defines coverage based on business risk, change impact, and critical flows](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l3-test-design-prioritization-01)

### Automation & Tooling
<a id="detail-l3-automation-tooling"></a>

[Exact source criteria](./qa-career-path.md#criteria-automation-tooling-l3-semi-senior-proficient-autonomous)

**Level snapshot:** Works independently with the approved stack

Consider whether the chosen automation gives maintainable, trustworthy evidence for recurring

### Performance Testing
<a id="detail-l3-performance-testing"></a>

[Exact source criteria](./qa-career-path.md#criteria-performance-testing-l3-semi-senior-proficient-autonomous)

**Level snapshot:** Designs scenarios and K6 scripts autonomously

Relate scenarios and results to expected product usage, response-time objectives, and customer
impact. A script passing is not itself a product-quality conclusion; interpretation and agreed
thresholds matter.

- [Designs performance scenarios autonomously from stories, DoR non-functional requirements and SLOs](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l3-performance-testing-01)

- [Selects and runs smoke, load, stress, spike or soak tests based on risk and release](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l3-performance-testing-03)
- [Analyses results, detects degradations and reports them with evidence](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l3-performance-testing-04)
- [Stops or escalates when SLOs are not met](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l3-performance-testing-05)

### Accessibility (A11y)
<a id="detail-l3-accessibility-a11y"></a>

[Exact source criteria](./qa-career-path.md#criteria-accessibility-a11y-l3-semi-senior-proficient-autonomous)

**Level snapshot:** Plans and runs manual and automated A11y, gives go/no-go

Connect checks to the people and journeys the product must support, and to the applicable
compliance evidence. Automated findings are useful but do not replace the manual checks
described by the source.

- [Covers web and app: keyboard, screen readers, zoom/responsive, contrast, forms](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l3-accessibility-a11y-02)
- [Automates A11y checks in the approved stack (Playwright + axe-core, Appium accessibility tree)](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l3-accessibility-a11y-03)
- [Applies the A11y severity matrix and gives go / no-go to PRE with evidence](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l3-accessibility-a11y-04)
- [Challenges missing A11y criteria in refinement and DoR](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l3-accessibility-a11y-05)

### AWS & Cloud Awareness
<a id="detail-l3-aws-cloud-awareness"></a>

[Exact source criteria](./qa-career-path.md#criteria-aws-cloud-awareness-l3-semi-senior-proficient-autonomous)

**Level snapshot:** Uses product architecture on AWS to define scope and risks

Use cloud knowledge to identify how architecture, environment differences, or asynchronous flows
change test scope. QA is expected to collaborate with specialists, not replace Cloud or
Architecture implementation ownership.

- [Understands the architecture of own product on AWS and uses it to define test scope and risks](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l3-aws-cloud-awareness-01)
- [Discusses cloud-related risks with Devs, TLs, Architecture and Cloud with confidence (asynchronous flows, timeouts, retries, configuration per environment)](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l3-aws-cloud-awareness-02)
- [Identifies when an issue is likely infrastructure or configuration rather than functional, and routes it correctly](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l3-aws-cloud-awareness-03)
- [Uses the AWS-based QA tools adopted by Iberia (e.g. AWS Device Farm) in daily work](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l3-aws-cloud-awareness-04)

### Observability (Dynatrace)
<a id="detail-l3-observability-dynatrace"></a>

[Exact source criteria](./qa-career-path.md#criteria-observability-dynatrace-l3-semi-senior-proficient-autonomous)

**Level snapshot:** Uses Dynatrace proactively every cycle, with evidence in go/no-go

Consider whether the team can use traces, dashboards, and signals to understand behaviour during
testing and make timely quality decisions. Observability should prevent surprises, not only
explain incidents after release.

- [Uses Dynatrace proactively in every test cycle: confirms new changes add no errors, latency or resource regressions before moving to the next environment](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l3-observability-dynatrace-01)
- [Correlates functional and performance test results with Dynatrace data (traces, services, databases, real user monitoring) to give root cause with evidence](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l3-observability-dynatrace-02)
- [Agrees with Devs the key signals and SLOs to watch for a story or release](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l3-observability-dynatrace-03)
- [Includes observability evidence in go/no-go decisions](./qa-career-path.md#qa-item-l3-observability-dynatrace-04)

## Purpose

Explain the technical influence, complex-scope ownership, and mentoring expected at L4 in terms
that product stakeholders can connect to delivery decisions.

## Audience

Product Owner, Product Manager, Delivery Lead, and Technical Lead. Backend Developer, Coach, and
Mentor use the detailed criteria for technical review.

## Overview

L4 drives technical quality in the squad and influences design and delivery beyond personal tasks.
The role includes owning architecture for complex scope, reviewing operational risks, and helping
peers improve. This is not simply L3 work delivered faster or with less supervision.

## Product-team context

For example, an L4 contribution could be guiding a change whose compatibility, database migration,
and message-processing behaviour create coordinated delivery risks. Stakeholders should receive
clear options, evidence for the recommendation, and an explanation of what must be monitored or
validated. The Developer should make trade-offs understandable without hiding uncertainty.

Mentoring and practice improvement should make the squad more capable, not more dependent on a
single expert. Reviews of performance, dependencies, security, and observability should influence
normal planning rather than appear only when an incident occurs.

## Evidence and progression

Look for documented trade-offs, improvements adopted by peers, mentoring outcomes, and operational
quality work that changed delivery decisions. A design document alone is not proof of influence;
the review should discuss its adoption and effect.

The [L4 gate and L4-to-L5 evidence](./Backend_Java_Career_Path_Chapter.md#l4-promotion-gate)
expect technical influence, standards, and sustained Senior impact across teams. Specialist
collaboration remains necessary; Backend ownership does not replace Cloud Engineer responsibilities.

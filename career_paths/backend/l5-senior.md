## Purpose

Describe sustained Senior Backend impact across products and suppliers, and help product
stakeholders distinguish organisational capability growth from individual expert delivery.

## Audience

Product Owner, Product Manager, Delivery Lead, and Technical Lead. Backend Developer, Coach, and
Mentor use the linked standards and criteria for technical calibration.

## Overview

L5 sets Backend standards, shapes capability growth, and acts as a reference across products and
suppliers. The expected impact extends beyond one squad: standards should improve consistency,
compatibility, security, and maintainability while allowing informed product-specific trade-offs.

## Product-team context

A representative contribution could align API compatibility or persistence practices across
products so teams can evolve safely without repeated integration failures. Stakeholders should
understand the problem being solved, the cost of adoption, and the consequences of exceptions.
A standard that exists only as a document does not establish cross-team impact.

The Senior should raise capability through guidance, mentoring, and supplier alignment rather
than become an approval bottleneck. Responsible AI use, cloud judgement, and testing strategy
belong within this wider decision model; introducing tools without assessed value or governance
does not demonstrate leadership.

## Evidence and calibration

Useful evidence includes adoption of standards across teams, reasoned handling of exceptions,
supplier alignment, and peers who can make better decisions without direct intervention.
Review sustained outcomes alongside technical validation; title, tenure, or visibility alone
cannot establish this level.

The [L5 gate](./Backend_Java_Career_Path_Chapter.md#l5-promotion-gate) identifies cross-team
reference status, alignment, standards, capability growth, and responsible AI literacy.
The supplied Academy material does not define progression to L6.

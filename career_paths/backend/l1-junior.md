# Backend Developer · L1 Junior

[Academy index](../README.md) · [Backend overview](./README.md) · [Next level](./l2-jr-advanced.md)

## Purpose

Explain the contribution and support needs of a Junior Backend Developer so that product
stakeholders can plan realistic assignments and provide evidence-based growth feedback.

## Audience

Product Owner, Product Manager, Delivery Lead, and Technical Lead. Backend Developer, Coach, and
Mentor use the linked criteria to review technical progress.

## Overview

L1 builds Java/Spring fundamentals and reliable delivery habits while working with guidance.
The person is learning how business rules become service behaviour, how changes are reviewed,
and how to protect existing functionality. Independent service ownership is not the expectation.

## Product-team context

A suitable learning situation could be a small, well-defined change to an existing endpoint or
business rule, with acceptance criteria and an available reviewer. This is an illustration, not
an additional requirement. Stakeholders should expect clear status updates, early questions,
and evidence that feedback is incorporated rather than repeated avoidable mistakes.

Plan review and coaching capacity into the work. A task completed with substantial guidance can
still demonstrate L1 progress; it should not be presented as evidence of independent delivery.
Conversely, asking for clarification or raising a concern about unsafe input handling is a
positive behaviour, not a reason to discourage escalation.

## Evidence and progression

Review completed changes together with unit tests, review comments, and examples of clear blocker
communication. Ask what the Developer understood and what support was needed, not just whether
the ticket closed. The source's [L1 gate and L1-to-L2 evidence](./Backend_Java_Career_Path_Chapter.md#l1-promotion-gate)
emphasise reliable delivery, testing, and basic stack understanding.

Experience ranges in the presentation are indicative. Stakeholders should discuss sustained
behaviour with the Technical Lead and Coach instead of treating elapsed months as promotion approval.

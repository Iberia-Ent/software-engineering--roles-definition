# Platform Engineer

## Role Description

The Platform Engineer is a key technical role responsible for designing, implementing, and evolving the tooling and processes related to the application lifecycle, DevSecOps practices, and the internal development platform. This role ensures that development teams have the infrastructure, tools, standards, and support they need to deliver high-quality software efficiently, securely, and at scale.

This role is especially focused on enabling and improving the software delivery lifecycle through GitHub Actions and AWS, while contributing to the adoption and evolution of the internal development platform based on Backstage. The Platform Engineer also helps drive innovation through the adoption of modern platform capabilities, including AI-powered tooling, agents, and automation, to improve developer experience, operational efficiency, and platform intelligence.

## Responsibilities

- **Platform Engineering**: Design, build, maintain, and improve platform capabilities that support development teams across the software delivery lifecycle.
- **Application Lifecycle Automation**: Implement, maintain, and optimize application lifecycle workflows using GitHub Actions, ensuring efficient, reliable, and standardized delivery processes.
- **Cloud Enablement**: Develop and operate platform solutions on AWS, following best practices for scalability, resilience, cost-efficiency, and security.
- **DevSecOps**: Integrate security controls, validation, and automation into delivery pipelines and platform services from the beginning of the development lifecycle.
- **Internal Developer Platform**: Contribute to the development, adoption, and continuous improvement of the Internal Developer Platform, with special focus on Backstage as a developer portal and enablement layer.
- **Tooling and Automation**: Evaluate, implement, and support tools and automation solutions that improve developer productivity, platform reliability, and operational consistency.
- **AI Enablement**: Explore and implement practical use cases for artificial intelligence in platform engineering, including AI assistants, agents, intelligent automation, and developer support capabilities.
- **Collaboration**: Work closely with development teams, QA, Cloud Engineers, DevOps Engineers, and other stakeholders to understand their needs and deliver platform solutions that improve their day-to-day work.
- **Operational Support**: Troubleshoot platform issues, support engineering teams, and ensure the smooth operation of pipelines, developer tooling, and platform services.
- **Observability and Reliability**: Implement monitoring, logging, alerting, and observability practices to ensure platform health, availability, and performance.
- **Documentation**: Create and maintain clear and comprehensive documentation for platform services, standards, workflows, and best practices.
- **Continuous Improvement**: Identify opportunities to improve platform performance, developer experience, governance, security, and automation maturity.
- **Compliance and Security**: Ensure platform services and processes align with security and compliance standards, applying the necessary controls and guardrails.

## What we expect from you

- **Experience**: Solid experience in platform engineering, DevOps, cloud engineering, or related fields, with hands-on involvement in software delivery platforms and automation.
- **GitHub Actions**: Strong experience designing, implementing, and maintaining GitHub Actions workflows for build, test, release, deployment, and operational automation across the application lifecycle.
- **AWS**: Strong knowledge of AWS services and architectures, with experience building and operating cloud-native solutions in secure and scalable environments.
- **Internal Developer Platform**: Experience with or solid understanding of Internal Developer Platforms, including concepts such as self-service, golden paths, developer enablement, service catalogues, and platform abstraction.
- **Backstage**: Practical knowledge of Backstage as a developer portal, including service catalogues, templates, plugins, and its role in improving developer experience and standardization.
- **DevSecOps**: Good understanding of DevSecOps principles, including security automation, secrets handling, policy enforcement, dependency management, and secure software delivery practices.
- **Infrastructure and Automation**: Experience with infrastructure as code, scripting, automation, containers, and deployment patterns commonly used in modern engineering platforms.
- **AI and Emerging Tooling**: Familiarity with artificial intelligence applied to engineering platforms, including AI agents, developer copilots, intelligent documentation assistants, automated troubleshooting support, knowledge retrieval systems, and workflow automation use cases that improve developer productivity and platform operations.
- **Problem Solving**: Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills, with the ability to solve technical issues in complex distributed environments.
- **Security and Compliance**: Knowledge of security best practices and compliance requirements within software delivery and platform operations.
- **Communication**: Good communication skills to collaborate effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- **Collaboration**: Ability to work collaboratively with cross-functional teams and stakeholders.
- **Continuous Improvement**: Commitment to continuous learning and improvement, staying current with industry trends and advancements in platform engineering, cloud, developer experience, and AI.
- **Ownership**: Proactive attitude and strong sense of ownership when managing platform services, delivery automation, and internal tooling.
